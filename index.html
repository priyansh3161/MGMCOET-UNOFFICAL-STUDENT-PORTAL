How to use

Get started in minutes

New here? Just follow these simple steps.

1

Create your account

Tap Create account and fill in:

  • Full name, email, branch, year and semester
  • Roll number (e.g. 2200290100XX)
  • A password (at least 6 characters)
  • Security keyword — it brings your chats back on a new phone or laptop. Remember it, it cannot be recovered.
2

Log in

Sign in with your roll number or email and password. Forgot your password? Tap Forgot password and we'll send a reset link (check your Spam/Junk folder too). Turn on Remember me to stay signed in.

3

Choose your class

On the dashboard, tap Change class and pick your branch and year. Your timetable, subjects and assignments are set up just for you.

4

Mark your attendance

Open Attendance and tap Present or Absent for each subject. Missed a day? Pick the date in the calendar.

  • Green dot = Present, red dot = Absent
  • It's your personal record and is not linked to the college system
5

Learn with Academics

Pick a subject → View Chapters → Watch Video. Videos resume right where you stopped, with speed control, quality options, 10-second skip and fullscreen. Notes, practice sets, lab manuals and PYQs are here too.

6

Explore the E-Library

Use the search bar or the tabs (Books, Notes, Syllabus, PYQ) to find what you need. Tap Contribute Resource to upload your own PDF (up to 100 MB). Share any resource with a link or in chat.

7

Chat in Community

Use the top-right icon to start a New Chat or New Group. Messages from new people land in Requests first — chat begins only after you accept. Block, mute and report are in the menu.

8

Confess, safely

Write your confession, pick a category, add a photo or video and hit Post. Your name is never shown. Delete your posts anytime from My Posts. Please be kind: no harassment and no sharing of personal details.

9

Turn on notifications

Go to Settings → Notifications and allow alerts to hear about new messages, assignments and notices. Dark mode lives in Settings → Preferences.

10

Need help?

Reach our Telegram Support Team below. Send a screenshot and your roll number and we'll sort it out fast.

Pro tip: Add the website to your phone's home screen. Open your browser menu and tap Add to Home screen — it opens just like an app.

FAQ

Quick answers

Is this the official college portal?

No. It's an unofficial portal built by students. Your attendance is your own log and isn't linked to the college system.

What is a security keyword?

Your chats are encrypted. This keyword lets you open your old chats on a new device. If you forget it, it can't be recovered — so save it somewhere safe.

I forgot my password. What now?

Enter your roll number or email on the login page and tap Forgot password. Check your Spam/Junk folder for the reset email.

Will my name show on confessions?

No. Posts are anonymous. Harmful content can still be reported and removed by moderators.

How do I get a staff account?

Staff access is approved by an admin. Message our Telegram support team with your name, department and college email.
